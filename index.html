🎸 AirGuitar

gesture-controlled virtual guitar

Show both hands to the camera.
Left hand — raise 1–4 fingers to pick a chord (1 = C, 2 = G, 3 = Am, 4 = F).
Right hand — sweep your index fingertip across the glowing line to strum.

1 finger — C Major 2 fingers — G Major 3 fingers — A Minor 4 fingers — F Major

Requires camera + audio permission. Works best in good, even lighting with hands clearly in frame.
